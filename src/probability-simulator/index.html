💡 Law of Large Numbers
The Law of Large Numbers states that as the number of trials increases, the observed (empirical) probability of each outcome converges toward its true (theoretical) probability. Run a small number of trials and you will see wild swings — run thousands and the results will settle close to the expected values.

Try it: flip the coin 10 times, then 10,000 times. Watch the bar chart even out and the convergence line home in on 50%.
