💡 The Golden Rule of Algebra
Whatever you do to one side of an equation, you must do to the other. The scale is a physical model of this rule — any operation that keeps both pans at the same height is a legal move. Your goal is to isolate x on one side by making a series of balanced moves. There is always more than one valid path to the solution.
